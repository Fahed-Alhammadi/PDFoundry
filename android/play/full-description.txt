PDFoundry is a simple, private PDF app. Open a document, do what you need, and send it on. Everything happens on your device.

READ
• Open PDFs from your files, email, browser or any app's share menu
• Search the text of a document
• Pinch to zoom
• Scan paper with the camera, or build a PDF from your photos

MERGE
• Select two or more documents and join them into one
• Put the files in the order you want before merging

EDIT
• Highlight with a marker
• Type text anywhere on a page
• Draw with your finger or a stylus
• Reorder, rotate, duplicate, extract and delete pages
• Add blank pages
• Undo your last changes

SIGN
• Draw your signature once and reuse it
• Choose black or blue ink
• Drag the signature onto the line, resize it, and place it
• Signed documents look the same in every PDF reader

ORGANISE
• Rename, duplicate, share, print and delete documents
• Sort and search your library

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• No ads and no analytics
• Nothing is uploaded: your documents and signatures never leave your device
• PDFoundry asks for no permissions
