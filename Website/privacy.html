Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

PDFoundry does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device. This policy covers PDFoundry for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux.

Your documents

PDFs you import, scan or create are stored only on your device: in PDFoundry's folder in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, and in the app's own storage on Android. On Android, a PDF you open with Open PDF stays where you keep it and changes are saved back to the same file; the app keeps a working copy in its own storage. On a computer, PDFoundry opens your files where you keep them and saves changes back to the same file; it keeps only a list of recently opened files. Nothing is ever uploaded. A document leaves the app only when you choose to share or print it.

Your signatures

Signatures you save are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are protected by the device's file encryption. You can delete them at any time in Settings > Signatures.

Camera

On iPhone and iPad, PDFoundry asks for camera access only when you tap Scan Document. The camera is used to scan paper into a PDF. Scans stay on your device.

On Android, PDFoundry has no camera permission. Scanning is done by the document scanner that is part of Google Play services on your phone, which hands the finished scan to PDFoundry. Google's own privacy policy applies to that scanner.

Photos

When you create a PDF from photos, you choose the photos in the system picker. PDFoundry receives only the photos you select and has no access to the rest of your library.

Analytics, ads and tracking

There are none. PDFoundry contains no analytics and no advertising. The phone apps make no network requests. The Android app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the network at all.

Updates on Mac, Windows and Linux

The desktop app checks for a new version about once a day, and when you ask it to in Settings. To do that it contacts GitHub, where PDFoundry's releases are published, and downloads the update from there. As with any website, GitHub sees your IP address when this happens; GitHub's privacy statement applies to that. PDFoundry sends nothing about you or your documents. This is the only time the desktop app uses the internet.

Deleting your data

Delete a document or signature in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
