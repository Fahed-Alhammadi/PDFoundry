Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

PDFoundry does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device.

Your documents

PDFs you import, scan or create are stored only on your device, in PDFoundry's folder in the Files app. They are never uploaded. A document leaves the app only when you choose to share or print it.

Your signatures

Signatures you save are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are protected by the device's file encryption. You can delete them at any time in Settings > Signatures.

Camera

PDFoundry asks for camera access only when you tap Scan Document. The camera is used to scan paper into a PDF. Scans stay on your device.

Photos

When you create a PDF from photos, you choose the photos in the system picker. PDFoundry receives only the photos you select and has no access to the rest of your library.

Analytics, ads and tracking

There are none. PDFoundry contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Deleting your data

Delete a document or signature in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
