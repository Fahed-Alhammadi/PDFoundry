New in this build: files open where they are.

• + > Open from Files opens a PDF from iCloud Drive or any folder without copying it into PDFoundry. It is listed with its location.
• Changes are saved automatically to that same file.
• More (…) > Revert to Original puts the file back as it was when you opened it, while the document is still open.
• Opening the same PDF twice no longer adds it twice.

Please try: open a PDF from Files or iCloud Drive, sign or add text, go back, then look at the file in the Files app. Also try deleting a page and then Revert to Original.
