New in this build: fill in forms, split PDFs and password protection.

• Fill & Sign now has Signature, Initials, Text, Date, Check and Cross.
• Fill in a PDF's own form fields: type in text fields, tick boxes, pick options, and tap a signature field to sign it. The values stay real form fields that other apps can change.
• Tools > Split PDF makes several PDFs from one: every page, every few pages, or ranges you type. The original is not changed.
• Tools > Password-protect locks a PDF with a password. In the reader, More > Protect with Password or Remove Password.
• A protected PDF can be edited after you enter its password, and keeps the password every time it is saved.

Please try: fill in and sign a PDF form, then open it in another app to check the fields. Split a document. Protect a file, close it, open it again with the password and edit it.
