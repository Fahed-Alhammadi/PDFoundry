New in this build: fixes for files opened where they are.

• Sharing a password-protected PDF keeps its password.
• Revert to Original now works for files opened from Files and iCloud Drive.
• Merging files opened from Files or iCloud Drive is more reliable.
• A file that can't be reached for a moment (signed-out cloud app, unplugged drive) is no longer removed from the list.
• Large files open without freezing the screen.

Please try: open a PDF from Files or iCloud Drive, sign or add text, then More (…) > Revert to Original. Also merge two PDFs opened from Files, and share a password-protected PDF.
