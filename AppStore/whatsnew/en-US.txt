New in this build: a new Tools tab and a clearer reader.

• Tools tab: Scan Document, Create from Photos, Merge PDFs and Extract Pages in one place.
• Extract Pages saves the pages you choose as a new PDF and never changes the original.
• The reader's bar now has labelled groups: Markup (highlight, underline, strikethrough, text, draw), Fill & Sign (signature, text) and Pages.
• After you place a signature the bar stays in Fill & Sign, so signing a second spot takes one tap.

Please try: Tools > Extract Pages on a file opened from Files, then check the original is unchanged. Sign a document in two places. Highlight, underline and strike through some text.
