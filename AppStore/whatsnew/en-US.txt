New in this build: marks you can change later, new markup tools and new tools.

• Every mark is now a real PDF annotation. Tap one later, even after reopening the file, to move, resize or delete it. Highlights, underlines and strikethroughs can be deleted but not moved. Other PDF apps show the marks.
• Markup has Shapes (rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow), Comment (sticky notes) and Redact: draw boxes or select text, then Apply. Each redacted page becomes an image, so the hidden text is really gone.
• New in Tools: Export as Images (PNG or JPEG), Compress, Redact, and a new Enhance section with Make Searchable (English and Arabic, offline), Page Numbers and Watermark. The last three are also in the reader's More menu.

Please try: move and delete a mark after reopening the file, redact a line and check it can't be found, make a scanned page searchable, and export pages, then Save Image from the share sheet.
