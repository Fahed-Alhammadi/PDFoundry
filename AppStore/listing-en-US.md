## Description

PDFoundry is a simple, private PDF app for iPhone and iPad. Open a document, do what you need, and send it on. Everything happens on your device.

READ
• Open PDFs from Files, Mail, Safari or any app's share sheet
• Search the text of a document
• Scroll pages vertically or sideways
• Scan paper with the camera, or build a PDF from your photos
• Make scanned pages searchable: text recognition reads English and Arabic, fully offline

MERGE
• Select two or more documents and join them into one
• Drag the files into the order you want before merging

EDIT
• Highlight in four colours, underline or strike through text
• Type text anywhere on a page
• Draw with your finger or Apple Pencil
• Reorder, rotate, duplicate, extract and delete pages
• Add blank pages
• Add rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows, and leave comments as sticky notes
• Every mark is a real PDF annotation that other PDF apps show. Tap it later, even after reopening the file, to move, resize or delete it
• Undo your last change
• Add page numbers or a watermark

REDACT
• Draw boxes over anything, or select text, then Apply
• Each redacted page becomes an image, so the hidden text is really gone from the file

EXPORT AND COMPRESS
• Save pages as PNG or JPEG images, at screen or print quality
• Make a smaller copy of a PDF; the text stays selectable

SIGN
• Draw your signature once and reuse it
• Choose black or blue ink
• Drag the signature onto the line, resize it, and place it
• Signed documents look the same in every PDF reader

ORGANISE
• Rename, duplicate, share, print and delete documents
• Your documents live in the PDFoundry folder of the Files app, so they are easy to back up

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• No ads and no analytics
• Nothing is uploaded: your documents and signatures never leave your device
• PDFoundry asks for the camera only when you tap Scan, and to add images to your photo library only when you tap Save Image after exporting pages

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** No. The app has no accounts.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

PDFoundry works fully on the device. It has no account, no server and makes no network requests.

How to test:
1. Launch the app. The Documents tab already holds a sample document, "Welcome to PDFoundry". No setup is needed.
2. Read: tap the document to open it. Tap the magnifying glass to search.
3. Markup: tap Markup in the bottom bar. Press and hold text to select it, then pick Highlight. Try Shapes (draw a rectangle), Comment, or Draw. Tap a placed shape later to move, resize or delete it.
4. Fill & Sign: tap Fill & Sign, then Signature. Draw a signature, use it, drag it into place and tap Place. Text, Date and the check mark work the same way.
5. Redact: in Markup choose Redact, drag a box over a line of text, then tap Apply. Search for a word from that line: it is gone.
6. Pages: tap Pages to reorder, rotate, duplicate, extract or delete pages.
7. Tools tab: Merge PDFs (first press and hold the sample in Documents and choose Duplicate, then pick both and tap Merge), Split PDF, Extract Pages, Compress, Export as Images, Protect with Password, Make Searchable, Page Numbers and Watermark.
8. Delete: in Documents, press and hold a document and choose Delete.

Permissions: the camera and adding to the photo library. The system asks for the camera only when the user taps "+" then "Scan Document", and for adding to the photo library only when the user taps Save Image in the share sheet after Tools > Export as Images. There is no onboarding or pre-permission screen. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which needs no permission.

**Contact:** fill in your name, phone and email in App Store Connect.
